# Placeholder gym photography

These are **stock photos standing in for real gym photography**. Every one of
them gets replaced by the gym's own images once that gym onboards — a
marketplace listing showing generic stock is a trust problem, not just a
cosmetic one.

## Why these particular images

Facility shots were chosen over photos of people wherever possible. Two
reasons, and the second is the important one:

1. A gym listing is selling its facilities, so equipment and interiors are
   simply more useful to a prospective member than stock models.
2. **The market is Kuwait.** Images of people in revealing athletic wear are a
   poor fit for the audience, and the brief flagged privacy expectations around
   the women's section as an open question. Until there is an explicit content
   policy, facility photography is the defensible default — particularly for
   `nawa-studio`, which is women-only.

That content policy is worth settling before launch, since it governs what
gyms are allowed to upload, not just what ships in this repo.
